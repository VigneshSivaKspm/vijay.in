1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
vijaykrishnan.in is a small personal website (5 public pages) for S Vijay
Krishnan: CEO of Youpeak Tech since 2023, film co-producer and chairman 2021-2023,
launching a new movie in 2027. It is a React + Vite single-page app that was
already pre-rendered at build time, so crawlers receive real HTML.

What changed in this SEO pass:
  * Performance: the home page went from 23.9 MB to about 1.3 MB transferred
    (mobile), layout shift from 0.282 to 0, and home-page mobile LCP from 3.7 s to
    0.8 s. Every page scores 100 for accessibility, best practices and SEO in
    the lab. Only /contact misses the LCP target on mobile (3.2 s). Details and
    caveats in section 19.
  * Wrong information removed from search-facing assets: the social-share image
    (og-image.jpg) still said "TAMIL FILM DIRECTOR & PRODUCER". It is regenerated.
  * New structured data (FAQPage, AboutPage+ProfilePage, ImageGallery, ContactPage
    with ContactPoint, Person with image and employer), an image sitemap, a real
    Privacy Policy page, a factual FAQ, and hosting configuration (vercel.json)
    for redirects, www/non-www, clean URLs and cache headers.
  * Analytics, conversion and Core Web Vitals tracking is built in, but OFF until
    you supply your own GA4 / GTM IDs.
  * Fonts are self-hosted (no Google Fonts request), 5 unused dependencies removed.

What was deliberately NOT done, and why (details in sections 9-11):
  * No pages or claims for the 30+ cities in the brief. Nothing in the project or
    in public search shows that Youpeak Tech or Vijay serves those places. The only
    verified location is Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Publishing the others would be
    exactly the kind of unverifiable claim this site has just been cleaned of.
  * No competitor analysis, keyword volumes or ranking claims: no keyword tool
    access, and a public search found no information on "Youpeak Tech".
  * No fake reviews, ratings, addresses, branches or certifications.

Rankings cannot be guaranteed. They depend on competition, authority, backlinks
and ongoing work.
